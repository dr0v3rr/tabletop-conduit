## Conduit v0.2.20

Track a Pokémon's experience in-app, and see which moves cost a bonus action or reaction.

### What changed

- **Experience tracker.** A poke5e Pokémon's sheet now shows an **EXP** row: its current experience,
  a progress bar toward the next level, and how much more is needed ("N to Lv X"). You can set the
  total directly or use the quick **"+ gained → Add"** to increment it as you earn XP; it writes back
  to poke5e when you hold the trainer's write key. At a threshold it shows "⭐ Ready to level up (on
  poke5e)", and "Max level" at 20. Leveling itself is still done on poke5e (HP roll / ASI / feats).
- **Action-economy tags on moves.** Moves that cost a **bonus action** or **reaction** are now flagged
  (BA / RXN) on a Pokémon's move list, read from poke5e's move data — previously only D&D Beyond
  spells showed this. Charge/recharge moves keep their ⏳ reminder.

All poke5e changes are scoped to poke5e sheets and don't affect D&D Beyond characters. No data-format
changes. Tests: 316 passing (adds an experience suite and action-economy coverage).
