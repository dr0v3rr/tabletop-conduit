## Conduit v0.2.35

Fixes a Charisma (and other ability) over-count for D&D Beyond characters built on the 2024 origin rules.

### What changed

- **Ungranted species ability increases no longer apply.** Under the 2024 rules, ability-score
  increases come from your **background**, not your species — so D&D Beyond leaves the old species
  bonus in the data flagged "not granted." Conduit was summing it anyway, inflating the score (e.g. a
  Gem Dragonborn's legacy **+2 Charisma** was being added, making every CHA roll **+2 too high**).
  Conduit now drops an ungranted *species* ability increase, matching D&D Beyond.

The fix is tightly scoped to that one case: granted species bonuses, and **feat/background ability
bonuses** (half-feats, your chosen ASI — which D&D Beyond applies even when it flags them "not
granted"), are all still counted, as are proficiencies. Verified against the DDB-matched golden
characters — every existing value is unchanged. Tests: 398 passing.
