## Conduit v0.2.18

poke5e move maths get STAB right, plus PP management (rest / Ether / Struggle) and a stickier Roll20 login.

### What changed

- **STAB is now applied to poke5e move damage.** A move whose type matches one of the Pokémon's own
  types now adds its Same-Type Attack Bonus (the proficiency bonus) to damage — previously it was
  computed but never added. This also fixes two moves whose flat bonus was being dropped: **Surging
  Strikes** (`+5`) and **Wicked Blow** (`+10`). Damage modifiers are now parsed generally
  (`MOVE` / `LEVEL` / `STAB` / flat numbers, in any combination), so a move like Confusion on a
  Psychic Pokémon reads `1dN + ability + STAB`.
- **Roll bonuses have hover tooltips.** To-hit, save DC, and damage on a Pokémon's moves now show a
  breakdown on hover, e.g. `+2 prof +1 WIS` or `1d8 +1 WIS +2 STAB`, so you can see exactly where a
  number comes from.
- **Struggle.** Every Pokémon now has the universal Struggle move (typeless, better of STR/DEX,
  `2 + MOVE`, no STAB/bonuses), usable any time — handy when everything's out of PP.
- **PP management.**
  - **Long Rest restores a Pokémon's PP** to full (writes back to poke5e when you hold the write key).
  - **Restore PP with Ether / Elixir / Leppa** via a new **🧪 PP** button: from a Pokémon's sheet it
    tops up its own moves; from a trainer's sheet you pick a team Pokémon — either way the picker
    shows each move's current/max PP, restores it, and consumes one item from the trainer's bag.
- **Roll20 / D&D Beyond logins survive restarts more reliably.** The persistent cookie store is now
  flushed to disk shortly after login and on quit, so a crash or force-quit no longer drops your
  session.

All poke5e changes are scoped to poke5e sheets and do not affect D&D Beyond characters. No
data-format changes. Tests: 306 passing (adds STAB/modifier, Struggle, and PP-item suites).
