## Conduit v0.2.14

Evolve your poke5e Pokémon from Conduit — pick the form, re-stat it, and spend the ASI, all in place.

### What changed

- **Evolve wizard.** Owned Pokémon (write key) now show an **✨ Evolve** button whenever the loaded
  form has an eligible target. The wizard mirrors poke5e's own flow: pick the target form (all edges
  are listed with their conditions), preview the re-stat (AC, hit die, types), confirm Max HP, and
  spend the evolution's Ability Score Increase — either by allocating points across the six scores
  (capped at 22, matching poke5e) or by taking a feat instead. Saving runs `update_pokemon` on the
  **same roster row**, so the creature becomes its evolved form without adding a duplicate entry.
- **Full evolution conditions.** The Pokédex now surfaces every poke5e edge condition —
  level, item, friendship/loyalty, gender lock, time of day, and known-move / move-type requirements
  — as readable labels (e.g. `Lv 8 · Water Stone`, `Lv 8 · Friendship · Night`, `Lv 8 · knows a
  Fairy move`). Hard, storable conditions (level, gender) gate the Evolve button; the rest are shown
  for the table to adjudicate.
- **HP on evolution.** Evolving keeps current HP (poke5e accumulates HP per level, so a bigger die
  only affects the *next* level-up). If an evolution coincided with a level-up, the wizard can roll
  `1d<new die> + CON` **on Roll20** — real dice on the VTT, attributed to the bound token or the
  owning trainer — and read the total back, or add the average with **+avg**.
- **ASI reminders.** Choosing a feat leaves a dismissible banner (Conduit can't add feats to poke5e,
  so you finish that there). Point-based increases are folded straight into the ability scores.
- **Species display + roll speaker.** Un-nicknamed Pokémon now show their proper-cased species name
  (e.g. **Ivysaur**, not `ivysaur`), and name-sync / HP rolls resolve the Roll20 *character* a token
  represents (e.g. `002 - Ivysaur`), not just the token label — so a mismatched character name gets
  reconciled and rolls attribute correctly.

Under the hood: the fast-uri dev dependency was bumped 3.1.5 → 3.1.7 (Dependabot).

No data-format changes. Tests: 278 passing (adds evolve-target normalization and in-place
`evolvePokemon` suites).
