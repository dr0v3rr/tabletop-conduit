## Conduit v0.2.31

A D&D Beyond currency bar, and a fix so attunement-only item bonuses (Cloak/Ring of Protection) only count when the item is actually attuned.

### What changed

- **D&D Beyond currency.** Load a D&D Beyond character and the Inventory section now shows a **Currency**
  bar — all five coins (PP/GP/EP/SP/CP) as editable fields, plus an **Amount + coin + Add / Remove**
  adjuster so you can add or remove *any* quantity at once (not just one at a time). Changes sync to
  D&D Beyond through its own coin-transaction API; it reads your live balance first and never lets a
  coin drop below zero. (Owned characters only; read-only sheets just show the totals.)
- **Attunement is respected for item bonuses.** Items whose bonus requires attunement — Cloak of
  Protection, Ring of Protection, and the like — were applying their +1 (to saves, skills, checks)
  even when the item was equipped but **not attuned**. D&D Beyond flags attunement on the bonus itself
  rather than the item, which Conduit's check missed. Now such a bonus only counts once the item is
  attuned, matching D&D Beyond. (After toggling attunement on D&D Beyond, re-sync the character in
  Conduit to see the change.)
- **Roll20 restore fix.** A campaign whose name slugifies to "sessions" is now restored on launch
  instead of being mistaken for the Roll20 login page.

Behind the scenes: the currency endpoint was taken from D&D Beyond's own request (verified with a live
round-trip), and the whole release went through an adversarial security/correctness review. Tests: 392
passing.
