## Conduit v0.2.42

### Every number checked against D&D Beyond

Six real characters (2014, 2024 and mixed-edition, single-class and multiclass) were compared value by
value with what D&D Beyond itself shows: ability scores, saves, skills, passives, initiative, speed,
every attack's to-hit and damage, spell attack and DC, and every spell's to-hit, DC and dice. Everything
both show now agrees. What was wrong is below.

### 2014 and 2024 rules, side by side

Characters really do mix editions (a 2014 subclass with 2024 feats, say), so Conduit now follows each
**feat and feature's own edition**, from the book D&D Beyond lists it in:

- **Great Weapon Master**: 2014 offers −5 / +10; 2024 offers +Proficiency Bonus damage — not both.
- **Sharpshooter** −5 / +10 only for the 2014 feat. **Charger** +5 (2014) or +1d8 (2024).
- **Great Weapon Fighting**: rerolls 1s and 2s (2014) or treats them as 3s (2024) — and only on a melee
  weapon held in two hands, not on spells, bows or a one-handed swing.
- **Dread Ambusher** 1d8 (2014) / 2d6 (2024), **Lifedrinker** +CHA (2014) / +1d6 (2024),
  **Remarkable Athlete**, **Assassinate** (2024 adds your Rogue level), **Divine Strike** each by edition.
- **2014 Divine Smite** is now in the spell list: 2d8 radiant, spends the slot, up-casts to 5d8.

Rules for the whole character follow the sheet: **Exhaustion** and the concentration DC cap use the
2024 rules unless your sheet recovers half Hit Dice on a long rest (the 2014 rule).

### Exhaustion levels

- The Exhaustion chip has **− / +** to set the level, synced to D&D Beyond.
- 2024: −2 per level to every d20 roll and −5 ft speed. 2014: disadvantage on checks, then on attacks and
  saves from level 3; speed halved at 2 and 0 at 5. Speed shows the reduced value.

### Fixed — wrong numbers

- **Speed** ignored speed modifiers (Mobile, Fleet of Foot, fly/swim/climb speeds, Unarmored Movement)
  and a speed typed onto the sheet.
- **Multiclass saving throws**: a second class's starting proficiencies (e.g. a Ranger's STR save on a
  Rogue-first character) were applied.
- **Ability scores with a 2024 background**: a legacy species' +2 was still applied when the background
  grants the increases, and Resilient's +1 was skipped.
- **Bonuses equal to an ability modifier** (Aura of Protection's CHA to saves, Dread Ambusher's WIS to
  initiative) counted as 0.
- **Oath and Domain spells** were missing — D&D Beyond keeps always-prepared spells outside the
  character, so Conduit now fetches them when loading.
- **Spell attack / save DC**: a focus item counted while unequipped or unattuned, and a class's focus
  (Bloodwell Vial, All-Purpose Tool) was added to spells from species, feats and options.
- **Eldritch Blast** now adds Agonizing Blast and fires one attack per beam. **Warlock spells** cast at
  your Pact slot level (Antagonize 6d4 from a 5th-level slot).
- **"Choose a type" damage** (Absorb Elements, Hunter's Mark) rolled every type at once — now one roll.
- **Rage** had no damage option (now +2/+3/+4 on Strength attacks). **Brutal Critical** added 1d6 to
  every hit (now extra weapon dice on a melee crit, automatically). **Divine Fury, Giant's Might,
  Brutal Strike, Divine Strike, Favored Foe** now scale with level.
- **Features you don't have yet** were offered (D&D Beyond lists every level's features), names matched
  too loosely ("Aura of Courage" offered Rage), and Tasha's optional features weren't recognised.
- **Polearm Master's** bonus attack now adds your ability modifier; a **customised weapon's** to-hit and
  damage edits now apply; only **equipped** weapons are listed as attacks.
- An **unequipped item's** resistances and spells no longer apply; item spells are tagged ITEM and free
  casts FREE, and neither spends a spell slot.
- 🎯 / 💀 now only mark attack rolls — not saves, checks, damage or healing.
