## Conduit v0.2.52

### poke5e moves roll what poke5e's sheet shows

Conduit read move data from an older poke5e file. poke5e's own sheet uses a newer source, and the two
disagreed on 34 attacking moves' dice (Aura Wheel 3d6 not 1d8, Overdrive 2d6 not 2d12, Axe Kick 2d10
not 2d6, and more). Conduit now uses the same data as poke5e's sheet: 2018 moves by default, or 2024 if
poke5e's "Preview Updated Moves" setting is on. The older file is only a fallback when the new one
can't be reached.

- **STAB follows your rules edition.** poke5e's default STAB is the levelling-table value under 2018
  rules and the proficiency bonus under 2024. Conduit always used 2024, so on a 2018 table on-type
  moves added too much (+5 instead of +3 at level 14). Conduit now reads the edition you picked in poke5e.
- **Move customization.** Custom to-hit, damage and DC bonuses, a custom type and custom move powers set
  in poke5e are now applied, as poke5e's sheet does. A customized move says so when you hover over it.
- **Crit ranges.** Moves that crit on 19–20 (Slash, Karate Chop and others), 17+ (Fickle Beam) or 16+
  (Frost Breath) now tell Roll20, so the card flags the crit.
- **Advantage and disadvantage.** Dig, Fly, Dive, Bounce, Phantom Force, Shadow Force, Feint Attack and
  Glaive Rush are rolled with advantage; Metal Burst and Revenge with disadvantage. These combine with
  your Adv/Dis setting, abilities and status effects the 5e way (one of each cancels).
- **Escalating moves.** Rollout and Outrage now roll real dice that grow with level, instead of the
  unrollable "Rd8". Magnitude shows its save DC and rolls its d100.
- **Moves that rolled the wrong thing or nothing:**
  - Electrify and Spore are CON saves, not attacks; Spiky Shield and Burning Bulwark no longer roll an
    attack; Focus Punch rolls an attack instead of auto-hitting.
  - Origin Pulse makes three attacks, Fire Blast two separate saves, Swords Dance two attacks from
    level 10.
  - Now rolled: Sing's hit-point pool, Spite's PP drain, Clangorous Soul's and Shed Tail's
    self-damage, Ingrain's healing, Harden's damage reduction and Draco Power's second damage.
  - Scale Shot adds MOVE to its first hit only. Weather Ball is labelled normal damage, Powder and
    Doom Desire typeless.
  - Healing moves (Milk Drink, Moonlight, Roost and others), Foul Play, Night Shade, Seismic Toss,
    Swift and Hyperspace Fury now scale with level as poke5e says.
- **Extra roll lines on the card** for a move's other dice: the status d6 for Dire Claw, Tri Attack
  and Secret Power, Hidden Power's damage-type d20, the d20 for a repeat Protect, Detect, Endure,
  King's Shield, Obstruct, Baneful Bunker or Wide Guard, and a few more.
- **Notes** for what can't be rolled now: damage that doubles each round, damage over time, bonus dice
  given to allies, and the "roll a d4 to hit again" moves (now worded to match the card).

### Status effects on a Pokémon's own rolls

poke5e's status rules now apply to the Pokémon's own checks and saves, not just Paralysis: Asleep gives
disadvantage on saves, Flinched on checks and saves, Poisoned on checks. A Flinched Pokémon's save moves
remind you that targets get advantage, and using a move while Confused shows your edition's rule. Under
2018 rules a Pokémon uses its trainer's initiative bonus.

### The poke5e bag

- **Use rolls and announces items properly.** A Potion's Use now rolls its 2d4 + 2 healing (it posted
  "Uses Potion" with no roll). Healing items with dice (Potions, Revives, Oran Berry, Energy Powder …)
  roll them; fixed heals (Sitrus Berry 30 HP, Max Potion 70 HP after 10 minutes …) say how much; every
  other item's card includes its effect.
- **Ethers, Elixirs and Leppa Berries** open the restore-PP picker with that item chosen, instead of
  taking one away without restoring any PP.
- **No Use on things you keep.** The Pokédex, rods, tents, kits, Key Stone and similar gear, held items,
  evolution stones and TMs no longer have a Use button that removes one (− still does).
- **Poké Balls** post their effect on the capture DC (Great Ball −5, Ultra Ball −10, Quick Ball −15 on
  the first round …). Several notes now match poke5e's wording, including both Timer Ball rules.
- The "Remaining" count on Use cards was one too low; hover text no longer mentions D&D Beyond on a
  poke5e sheet.

### poke5e compatibility

The compatibility check now covers both poke5e move sources (and that every move's dice are a kind
Conduit knows) and, with a trainer link, the move-customization fields.

Tests: 505 passing.
