## Conduit v0.2.24

Adding Pokémon works again, Poké Balls throw with one click, and bonus-action moves stand out.

### What changed

- **Fixed "Couldn't add Pokémon" (poke5e `add_pokemon` 404).** poke5e changed its add-Pokémon API
  signature, which broke adding a caught Pokémon to your roster (an HTTP 404). Conduit now sends the
  current parameters — the skill flags were renamed (`_rank_*` → boolean `_prof_*`) and two dropped
  fields (`_type`, `_abilities`) removed — so catching and adding a Pokémon writes to your poke5e
  team again.
- **Poké Ball "Throw" rolls directly.** Throwing a ball from your bag no longer opens the pick-a-
  target card — it posts your **Animal Handling** catch roll straight to Roll20 (honoring your current
  Advantage/Disadvantage setting), includes the ball's name (and, for **Safari / Friend / Sport**
  balls, your sheet-derived Nature / Persuasion / Athletics reduction the GM can't compute), and
  **decrements that exact ball**. Master Ball posts an automatic catch. The Pokédex "Catch…" flow still
  uses the full card when you want to record a specific species.
- **Bonus-action moves are easy to spot.** The **BA** tag on bonus-action moves is now a distinct
  filled badge instead of sharing the muted style of the Reaction / charge tags. A full review
  confirmed all 39 bonus-action moves are correctly identified and labeled.

No data-format changes — existing archives, stats, notebooks, and settings carry over. Tests: 354
passing.
