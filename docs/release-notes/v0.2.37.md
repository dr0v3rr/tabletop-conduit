## Conduit v0.2.37

A batch of quality-of-life improvements across rolling, poke5e, monster-running, and the app shell.

### Rolling

- **Roll results now read back for every roll.** The “✦ total 🎯/💀” summary only ever appeared for
  D&D Beyond sheet rolls; it now surfaces for poke5e and monster rolls too (it was matching on a field
  Roll20 leaves blank for those). You see the total and crit/fumble instead of the raw command.
- **Crit damage button (💥 Crit).** A one-shot toggle in the roll row: arm it and your next attack rolls
  its damage dice **doubled** (PHB crit). Clears after the roll. (The doubling was always built in — now
  there’s a way to trigger it.)
- **Hold-to-modify advantage.** **Alt-click** any rollable for one-off **advantage**, **Shift-click** for
  **disadvantage** — just for that roll, without touching the sticky Adv/Dis control.

### poke5e

- **Four more Poké Balls** — Premier, Luxury, Heal, and Dream — now show their own art in the bag and the
  catch window, and **Dream Ball** posts its “−5 vs a sleeping Pokémon” effect. (24 balls in total.)
- **Simpler catch window.** The ball / advantage / level pickers have been removed for now — a throw uses
  your first Poké Ball and a straight Animal Handling check, and records the catch at the species’
  minimum level. (The GM still applies the ball/SR/level DC reduction from the posted ball name.)
- **“Sleep move” Pokédex filter** — narrow the dex to species that learn a sleep move (pairs with the Dream Ball).

### Monsters / GM

- **Breath weapons and other save-based actions now load as rollable attacks** for Open5e creatures
  (e.g. a dragon’s Fire Breath), matching how D&D Beyond monsters already work.
- **Monster speed** is read from the creature’s actual movement data (walk/fly/swim/…) instead of a flat 30.
- **Monster search** tells a connection failure apart from a genuine “no matches,” so an outage no longer
  looks like a missing creature.

### App

- **The window remembers its size and position** (restored only when it still lands on a connected
  display) and has a sensible minimum size so the tabletop pane can’t be squeezed away.
- **Notebook page delete needs two clicks** (the first arms it) so one misclick can’t wipe a page.
- **Keyboard:** Cmd/Ctrl+F focuses the sheet’s filter; Esc clears it, or closes the Notebook.
- **The update prompt** now shows a short “what’s new” excerpt, and download progress appears in the title bar.

Pure logic stays unit-tested; this release adds coverage for the new ball art and the breath-weapon parsing. Tests: 411 passing.
