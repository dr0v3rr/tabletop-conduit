## Conduit v0.2.50

### Used-up items leave the bag

- **poke5e bag:** when an item reaches ×0 (Use, −, Throw, or a PP item), it is removed from the bag on
  poke5e, just like poke5e's own remove button. For 8 seconds its row reads "used up · removed from bag"
  with an **Undo** button that puts one back. Old ×0 rows already in a bag are hidden.
- **D&D Beyond:** nothing is deleted from your DDB inventory. Emptied consumables move into a collapsed
  **Used up (N)** group at the bottom, where + restocks them. A search opens the group when it matches
  something inside.

### Items sorted A–Z

The bag, D&D Beyond inventory and held items are listed alphabetically (case-insensitive, with numbers
in natural order).
