## Conduit v0.2.21

A per-campaign **Notebook**, a nostalgic **Red Pokédex** that pops up over the table, masked
undiscovered species, and a GM-friendly rework of the **catch** flow.

### What changed

- **📓 Notebook (all sources).** A new Notebook button in the top toolbar opens free-form notes tied
  to the **Roll20 campaign currently open** (`window.campaign_id`). Keep multiple named pages per
  campaign — session recaps, NPCs, clues, loot — with light formatting (bold / italic / list /
  heading); edits **autosave** and persist per campaign under `~/.conduit/notebook/<campaign>.json`
  (macOS/Linux) or `%APPDATA%\Conduit\notebook\` (Windows). Open a different game and the notes
  rebind to it. The 📁 button reveals the folder.
- **🔴 Red Pokédex.** The poke5e Pokédex is reskinned as the classic red handheld device — blue lens,
  status LEDs, and twin green LCD screens (species list + entry). It's launched from a small red
  **Pokédex-icon button** to the right of the tabs and now opens as a **popup over the table**: the
  VTT stays visible and dimmed behind the floating device instead of taking over the window. Close
  with the ✕, `Esc`, or a click outside.
- **Undiscovered species are masked.** Any species you haven't **Seen** or **Caught** shows as a
  silhouette with its name hidden behind `?????????` — no types, SR, stat block, abilities, moves, or
  evolution until you've encountered it. **Search still matches the real name**, so you can look one
  up and mark it Seen/Caught (or catch it) to reveal the entry.
- **Catch flow — the GM owns the DC.** Throwing a Poké Ball now posts a **raw Animal Handling roll**
  plus the **ball's name** to Roll20; the DC (and its SR / level / target-HP / ball reductions) stays
  with the GM and is **no longer shown to the player**. **Safari / Friend / Sport** balls additionally
  post your **Nature / Persuasion / Athletics** modifier — sheet values the GM can't see — so they can
  apply that reduction. Advantage stays a player-toggled checkbox whose tooltip lists the conditions
  (poisoned, restrained, asleep, burning, confused, paralyzed, frozen). Master Ball still auto-catches,
  and you still can't catch a Pokémon above your trainer level.
- **Caught from anywhere.** The **Caught** button is back next to Seen for every trainer, so a catch
  made outside the Pokédex (a Poké Ball thrown from the inventory **Use** button, or a narrative
  recruit) can be added to your roster/PC straight from the dex.

No data-format changes — existing archives, stats, and settings carry over. Tests: 316 passing.
