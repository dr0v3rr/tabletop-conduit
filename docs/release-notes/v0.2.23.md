## Conduit v0.2.23

Up-cast your spells, see your full slot ladder, and pact weapons now use the right ability.

### What changed

- **Spell up-casting.** Leveled damage spells now have a slot-level picker: cast Fireball in a 6th-
  or 9th-level slot and the damage scales accordingly (8d6 → 11d6 → 14d6). Conduit rolls the scaled
  damage, spends the slot you chose on D&D Beyond, and labels the Roll20 card with the level it was
  cast at. The per-slot increment is read from the spell's own data, so it's correct per spell.
- **A persistent Spell Slots strip.** Spellcasters get an always-visible slot ladder at the top of
  the sheet — every level 1–9 plus Pact Magic — sitting right above the Roll (advantage/disadvantage)
  controls. It no longer only appears next to levels you happen to know a spell at, so your high-level
  slots are finally visible (and usable via up-casting). Non-casters don't see it at all.
- **Pact weapons use the right ability.** Weapons granted a spellcasting-ability substitution — the
  Hexblade's Hex Warrior, Pact of the Blade, Kensei, and the like — now use that ability (Charisma
  for a Hexblade) for **both** the attack and the damage roll, whenever it's the higher modifier and
  the character is proficient. This reads D&D Beyond's own modifier, so it works for any such
  character, not a special case.
- **poke5e move-coverage fixes.** Closed 72 gaps where a move rolled the wrong thing: healing moves
  now roll healing (Milk Drink, Moonlight…), fixed-damage moves post their flat value with no stray
  STAB (Seismic Toss, Sonic Boom, Night Shade, Foul Play), a handful of status/utility moves stopped
  rendering bogus to-hit/damage, and more multi-hit moves fan into per-hit cards. Moves whose exact
  mechanic the app can't auto-apply now carry a short reminder note.

No data-format changes — existing archives, stats, notebooks, and settings carry over. Tests: 354
passing (adds up-cast damage-scaling, higher-level increment parsing, pact-weapon ability
substitution, and poke5e move-mechanics suites).
