## Conduit v0.2.43

### Advantage and disadvantage from your sheet now reach the roll

- **Armour that hinders Stealth** (half plate, plate and the like) now gives your Stealth rolls
  disadvantage while it's equipped, as on D&D Beyond.
- **Advantage or disadvantage your sheet always has** on a save or skill (e.g. Vedalken Dispassion's
  advantage on INT, WIS and CHA saves) was read but never applied — those rolled as normal rolls. They're
  now rolled with two dice, and combine the 5e way with the Adv / Dis buttons: advantage plus
  disadvantage is a normal roll. Situational ones ("while in sunlight") still aren't applied automatically.
- Saves and skills affected show an **ADV** / **DIS** tag; hover it to see where it comes from.
