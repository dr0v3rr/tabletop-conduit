## Conduit v0.2.45

### Windows: updating no longer logs you out of Roll20

Choosing **Restart now** after an in-app update starts the installer, which closes Conduit after about
a second — and it was doing so while Conduit was still saving your login to disk. Conduit now finishes
saving your cookies and site data **before** handing over to the installer, then exits straight away, so
your D&D Beyond and Roll20 sign-ins carry over. A normal quit now saves site data too, not just cookies.

**Updating to this version:** the update *into* v0.2.45 still runs the old code. To keep your login this
time, close Conduit normally first, then run `Conduit-Setup-0.2.45.exe` yourself. From v0.2.45 on,
**Restart now** is safe.
