## Conduit v0.2.10

The catch loop: throw a Poké Ball, roll, and add the Pokémon to your poke5e roster.

### What changed

- **Catch card.** A shared catch flow, launchable two ways:
  - From the **Pokédex** detail (**🎯 Catch…**), with the species preset.
  - From the **inventory** — **Throw** on any Poké Ball opens the same card with that ball preset and a **target species** search (the dex dataset loads on demand).
- **It's a roll builder, not a DC calculator.** You won't know the target's HP or DC at the table — the GM does. The card shows your **Animal Handling catch roll** with its modifiers:
  - **Ball** — each Poké Ball's DC reduction becomes a bonus to your roll (Great +5, Ultra +10, Safari + your Nature mod, …; Master Ball = automatic catch; situational balls are noted).
  - **Advantage** toggle (for a status) and an **Other** ad-hoc bonus.
  - **🎲 Throw** posts the actual roll (`[[1d20 + …]]`, or `2d20kh1` with advantage) to Roll20 and **consumes one ball** — written back to poke5e when you hold the write key, local otherwise. The **GM adjudicates**.
- **Caught = your poke5e roster.** **✔ Add to poke5e** creates the Pokémon on your trainer via `add_pokemon` (at the level you set, stats/ability/proficiencies from the species). It then shows **● Caught · on poke5e**. Read-only trainers fall back to a local caught tick. **Seen** stays a manual, persisted flag.
- **Rules enforced.** Can't catch a Pokémon whose level exceeds your trainer's; ball counts and the roster update live.
- **Inventory write-back.** poke5e bag quantities now persist via `update_inventory_item` when you hold the write key (they were previously local-only).

No data-format changes. Tests: 256 passing (adds an `add_pokemon` param-mapping suite).
