## Conduit v0.2.12

Correct Pokémon movement speeds, a multi-speed display, and safer per-Pokémon removal.

### What changed

- **Pokémon speeds are now correct.** A loaded Pokémon's speed was reading a field that doesn't exist on the poke5e row, so **every** Pokémon showed a hardcoded 30 ft. Speed is now derived from the species (where poke5e actually stores it). Validated across all 1,151 species — **~65% were previously wrong** (e.g. Snivy 25, Diglett 20 burrowing, Magnemite 20 hover, Magikarp 5).
- **Multiple movement modes.** 513 species have more than one mode (walking / climbing / swimming / flying / hover / burrowing; up to four — Koraidon). The **Speed** vital is now clickable when a creature has multiple modes: click to expand from the primary number to the full breakdown (e.g. `25 · climb 25`), with all modes in the tooltip. The preference persists.
- **Remove is Pokémon-aware; Delete is trainer-only.** When a **Pokémon** is selected (not the trainer):
  - **🗑 Delete is hidden** — it only ever made sense for the whole trainer.
  - **✕ Remove now removes that Pokémon** from the trainer (poke5e's `remove_pokemon`), behind a confirmation, and only when you own the trainer. Previously Remove/Delete on a Pokémon acted on the entire trainer.

No data-format changes. Tests: 273 passing (adds a movement-speed suite and a `remove_pokemon` param check).
