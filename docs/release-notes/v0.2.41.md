## Conduit v0.2.41

### Rests that match D&D Beyond (2024 rules)

**Short rest**
- Hit Point Dice are listed **per class**, just like D&D Beyond's Short Rest pane (e.g. Sorcerer d6 and
  Warlock d8), each with its heal formula.
- Click a die to spend it: it rolls in Roll20 and Conduit heals you by **exactly that result** — the same
  number everyone at the table sees. A Periapt of Wound Closure doubles it.
- **Short rest** then records the spent dice on D&D Beyond **without D&D Beyond rolling and healing a
  second time**. (Its "Automatically apply healing with dice result" option is switched off just for that
  save and switched back on afterwards.) Conduit checks D&D Beyond actually saved them, and if only some
  were saved, pressing Short rest again records just the rest.

**Long rest**
- Now uses D&D Beyond's own **Take Long Rest**, so everything a long rest does happens on your sheet:
  full HP, spell slots, Pact Magic, limited-use features, Exhaustion, and Hit Point Dice — **all** of them
  under the 2024 rules, or half if your sheet is set to the 2014 ("5e") rule. Conduit then reloads the
  character.
- If you'd spent Hit Point Dice without finishing the short rest, that short rest is recorded first.
- You need at least 1 HP to start a long rest. Read-only and offline sheets apply the same rules locally.

### Fixed

- **Short rests weren't saving to D&D Beyond at all.** Its rest buttons need a confirming second click,
  which Conduit wasn't giving. Spending several dice from one class at once could also lose some of them.
- **Ability scores for some older species** — a species' chosen +1s (e.g. Half-Elf) were being dropped,
  leaving those scores 1 too low (and every roll, Hit Die and max HP built on them). They're now only
  dropped when the background grants the ability increases, as the 2024 origin rules do.
