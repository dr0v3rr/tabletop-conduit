## Conduit v0.2.47

### Clearer Pokémon move damage

A move's damage tooltip now always lists its MOVE ability, even when that modifier is +0 — e.g.
Disarming Voice on a CHA 10 Pokémon reads **1d4 +0 CHA +2 STAB** instead of just "1d4 +2 STAB", which
made it look as if MOVE had been left out. The rolled damage is unchanged.

Tests: 473 passing.
