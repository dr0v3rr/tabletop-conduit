## Conduit v0.2.9

A full in-app Pokédex, plus trainer path/specialisation and a couple of quality-of-life fixes.

### What changed

- **📕 Pokédex (poke5e).** A new **Pokédex** tab (next to Table / poke5e) browses every poke5e
  species — 1,139 official — with real sprites and artwork:
  - **Filter** by name, region, SR band, collection state, and type; **fakémon** (community/custom
    species) are hidden behind a **Show fakémon** toggle.
  - **Detail** shows the stat block + ability scores, **abilities with their full text** (each with a
    📖 Display-in-VTT button), the **move list** (also 📖-enabled), the **evolution chain**, and a
    **Catch card**: `DC = 10 + ⌊SR⌋ + wild level` against your trainer's Animal Handling, a
    **Roll capture** button, and 📖 Display species.
  - **Seen** is a manual, **persisted** encounter flag — you mark it when your DM confirms you've come
    across a species in the VTT. **Caught** is derived automatically from your **trainer's team**.
  - Opens full-window; the tab is **only available for the poke5e character source**.
- **Trainer path & specialisation.** Loading a poke5e trainer now surfaces their **Path** (e.g. Guru,
  with its rank features as they unlock) and **Specialisation(s)** (e.g. Dragon Tamer, with stack
  count and effect) in Features & Abilities. Skill proficiency now also reads poke5e's **rank** so
  **expertise** doubles correctly.
- **Initiative moved.** Initiative is now a **⚡ Init** quick-roll up in the Roll controls (next to
  Whisper / Reroll) instead of under Saving Throws — available on every character sheet.
- **Window title** is now **`Conduit v<version>`** (was "D&D Beyond ↔ Roll20").

No data-format changes — existing archives, stats, and settings carry over. Tests: 253 passing
(adds Pokédex normalizer, trainer-extras, and card-sanitizer suites).
